# Photographer Portal - Customer User Manual

Welcome! This guide will help you understand how to easily book a photographer, track your request, and manage your payments through our portal.

## Overview
As a customer, your journey consists of two main parts:
1. **The Booking Page:** Where you submit your initial request.
2. **The Tracking Page:** A personalized portal to monitor your booking, chat with your photographer, and make secure payments.

---

## Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. Booking a Photographer
When you visit a photographer’s public booking page, follow these steps to send a request:
*   **Select a Date:** Choose your preferred event date from the interactive calendar. Unavailable dates will be greyed out.
*   **Event Details:** Fill in the event's start and end times, event type (e.g., Wedding, Birthday), and location details (City, District, and Google Maps link if available).
*   **Your Information:** Provide your Name, Email, and Phone Number. Add any specific notes or requirements for the photographer.
*   **Submit:** Click the submit button. Your request will be sent to the photographer for review.

### 2. Accessing Your Tracking Page
Once you submit a request, a unique tracking link will be generated for you. You will also receive an email with this link. 
*   **Save this link!** It is your private portal to manage this specific booking.
*   You do not need to create an account or remember a password; your tracking link gives you direct access.

### 3. Understanding Booking Statuses
On your tracking page, you will see a progress tracker with the following stages:
*   **PENDING:** The photographer is reviewing your request.
*   **PROPOSED:** The photographer has sent you a quotation (packages and pricing). You have 24 hours to review and pay the deposit.
*   **CONFIRMED:** You have paid the deposit. The photographer is officially booked for your date!
*   **COMPLETED:** The event is finished, and the final payment has been settled.

### 4. Reviewing Proposals & Paying Deposits
When a photographer accepts your request, they will send a proposal:
*   Open your tracking link (Status will be **PROPOSED**).
*   Review the packages offered, any notes from the photographer, and the required deposit amount.
*   Click **Pay Deposit** to proceed to our secure Stripe checkout.
*   After successful payment, your booking is instantly **CONFIRMED**.

### 5. Chatting with Your Photographer
You can communicate with your photographer directly through the tracking page:
*   Click the **Chat** icon or section on the tracking page.
*   Send messages to discuss event details, ask questions, or request changes.

### 6. Final Payments & Invoices
*   **Final Payment:** The remaining balance is usually settled closer to the event or on the day itself. Your photographer will update the system once this is settled.
*   **Invoices:** Once your booking is fully paid and marked as **COMPLETED**, you can download your official PDF invoice directly from your tracking page. You will also receive a copy via email.

---
**Need Help?** If you have any technical issues with the tracking link, please contact your photographer, and they can re-share the link with you.
