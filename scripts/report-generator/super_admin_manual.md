# Photographer Portal - Super Admin User Manual

Welcome to the Super Admin Control Panel! This guide outlines how to manage the platform, oversee photographer accounts, and review system-wide analytics.

## Overview
The Super Admin dashboard provides a bird's-eye view of the entire platform. It is designed to help you efficiently manage users and monitor system health.

The dashboard consists of four main sections accessible from the side menu:
1.  **Overview**
2.  **User Management**
3.  **Reports & Analytics**
4.  **Profile Settings**

---

## Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. The Overview Dashboard
When you log in, you will land on the Overview page.
*   **System Status Cards:** Quickly view key metrics such as Total Active Photographers, Pending Registrations, Total Revenue Processed, and Active Bookings.
*   Use this screen to gauge the daily activity and health of the platform at a glance.

### 2. User Management (Managing Photographers)
As a Super Admin, you are responsible for onboarding new photographers to maintain platform quality.
*   Navigate to the **User Management** tab.
*   Here you will see a list of all registered photographers, categorized by their account status (Pending, Active, Suspended).
*   **Approving Accounts:** When a new photographer registers, their account is "Pending." Review their details and click **Approve** to grant them access to their dashboard and public booking page.
*   **Suspending Accounts:** If a photographer violates platform rules, you can **Suspend** their account. This hides their public booking page and restricts their login access.
*   **Viewing Details:** Click on any photographer to view their full profile, contact information, and activity history.

### 3. Reports & Analytics
Monitor the financial and operational success of the platform:
*   Navigate to the **Reports & Analytics** tab.
*   **Financial Reports:** View platform-wide revenue summaries, including successful deposit transactions and total volume processed through the system.
*   **Location Analytics:** Access maps and charts showing the most popular regions and cities for bookings. This helps in understanding market demand and planning marketing strategies.
*   **Usage Stats:** Track how many bookings are being processed daily/weekly across the entire platform.

### 4. Profile Settings
*   Navigate to the **Profile Settings** tab.
*   Update your Super Admin name, email, and password to ensure your administrative account remains secure.

---
**Tip:** Regularly check the "Pending Registrations" metric on your Overview dashboard to ensure new photographers are onboarded without delay!
