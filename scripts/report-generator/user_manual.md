# Photographer Portal - Reservation Management User Manual

Welcome to the Photographer Portal! This manual will guide you through managing your reservations efficiently using the dashboard.

## Overview
The **Reservations Tab** on your dashboard allows you to view, propose, reject, and manage payments for client booking requests. The screen is divided into two sections:
1. **Left Pane (Reservation List):** A list of all your reservations. You can search, sort, and filter them by status.
2. **Right Pane (Reservation Details):** A detailed view of the selected reservation where you can take actions like sending a proposal, logging a payment, or rejecting the request.

## Reservation Statuses
*   **PENDING:** A new request from a client waiting for your response.
*   **PROPOSED:** You have sent a package quotation to the client.
*   **CONFIRMED:** The client has accepted your proposal and paid the deposit. The event is officially booked.
*   **COMPLETED:** The event is finished, and the full payment has been settled.
*   **REJECTED / CANCELED:** You rejected the request, or the client canceled it.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. Viewing New Requests
*   Go to the **Reservations** tab on your dashboard.
*   Look for reservations with the **PENDING** status in the left list.
*   Click on a reservation to view its details on the right side.
*   Review the client's information, event date, notes, location details, and map link in the **Customer Details Card**.

### 2. Proposing a Quotation (Accepting a Request)
To accept a **PENDING** request, you must send a proposal:
*   In the right pane, locate the **Propose Quotation** section.
*   **Select Packages:** Choose one or more of your existing packages to offer, or click **Create Custom Package** to offer a tailored service and price.
*   **Set Deposit:** Specify the required advance deposit amount for the selected packages.
*   **Add a Note:** (Optional) Write a message to the client.
*   Click **Send Proposal**.
*   *Important:* Sending a proposal automatically locks your calendar for that date for 24 hours. The client has 24 hours to pay the deposit. The status will update to **PROPOSED**.

### 3. Rejecting a Request
If you are unavailable or cannot fulfill the request:
*   Select the reservation and click **Reject Request**.
*   Provide a short reason for the rejection.
*   Confirm the action. The status will update to **REJECTED**, and the client will be notified.

### 4. Updating a Proposal
If a client requests changes before paying the deposit:
*   Select the **PROPOSED** reservation.
*   Adjust the selected packages, deposit amount, or notes.
*   Click **Update Proposal**. This will reset the 24-hour calendar lock.

### 5. Managing Payments & Completing a Booking
Once the client pays the deposit online, the status automatically changes to **CONFIRMED**.
*   Select the confirmed reservation to view the **Proposal Status Summary**. Here you can see the total amount, the deposit paid, and the remaining balance.
*   **Logging Cash Payments:** If the client pays the remaining balance offline (e.g., in cash) on the day of the event, click **Log Manual Cash Payment**.
*   Confirming this action will mark the booking as fully settled. The status will change to **COMPLETED**, and an invoice will be automatically emailed to the client.
*   You can also click **Download Invoice** at any time for your records.

---
**Tip:** Use the search bar and status dropdown filters on the left pane to quickly find specific bookings!
